## Lore cross-checks and adaptation decisions

- The Wraith’s two exhibits form one sixth card; Lost and Found are two halves of one second-deck card. [Dark Carnival overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_Carnival_%28Insane_Clown_Posse%29).
- Riddle Box uses a consequential reveal. Collapsing symbol tiles and shoving are our game interpretation. [Riddle Box](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riddle_Box).
- Milenko’s illusion/temptation theme informs deceptive prizes. Breakable mirrors and hostile reflections are new game mechanics. [The Great Milenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_Milenko).
- Jack the Sinister and Jake the Just juggle sins; competitive fireball deflections adapt that idea. The playable spirit is Jack, not a combined character. [The Amazing Jeckel Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Amazing_Jeckel_Brothers).
- Fearless Fred Fury’s fight-back framing was cross-checked against an artist interview. The recharge rule is an adaptation. [Artist interview](https://aestheticmagazinetoronto.com/2019/02/07/interview-insane-clown-posse-fearless-fred-fury-juggalo-weekend-wwe/).

Wikipedia is a secondary lore cross-check, not a substitute for approved liner notes or artist review. Actual cover imagery and the label catalog are the primary visual and discographic references.

## What the build does and does not claim

The character names and cover images are actual ICP material. The full-body spirit designs, arenas, actions, rules, and story framing are generated interpretations for this concept. Literal chicken catching is a comic gameplay reference to the song title, not a claim about the song’s meaning. No lyrics, song recordings, or performer voice clones are embedded. The synthesized beat and effects are original sketches.

The archive is a researched starting point, not an exhaustive discography or evidence of commercial rights. Making an official release remains a separate partnership and production step. The game plan lists the broader catalog work still needed.
