Movement review

Walking frames for the ground characters and creatures. Slow the loop or pause on each pose to inspect leg changes, body consistency, and the planted foot. These are prototype cycles, ready for further animation cleanup.

Animation standard: different leg poses, clear contact and passing phases, opposing arm movement, a consistent scale, and a stable foot baseline. In the game, character footsteps advance with distance traveled. Spirit characters float and are reviewed separately.
